About

I'm a Software Engineering student at the University of Sialkot in Pakistan, building ML systems alongside coursework rather than waiting until after it. Most of what I know came from shipping something and then finding out what I got wrong.

My main interest is evaluation: how you tell whether a model is reasoning or pattern matching. That's the thread running through CognitivePrism, which is where most of my time goes right now. The rest of my work is applied, data pipelines, backends, and AI products that need to survive contact with real users.

I care about benchmarks that measure something real, models that do something useful, and code someone else can read six months later.

Selected projects

CognitivePrism

In progress

An adversarial benchmark for testing whether frontier models hold up under epistemic pressure. v2.0 runs 604 items across five tracks, invented phonological grammar, epistemic archaeology, adversarial camouflage, constraint lattices, and institutional cognition, scored by seven functions built for the Google DeepMind × Kaggle benchmarking competition.

I'm currently expanding coverage to 50+ models, with the goal of submitting to the NeurIPS Evaluations & Datasets track in 2027. Open to working with a research lab on the scale-up.

Python LLM evaluation Benchmark design NLP

A production chatbot built during my internship at Zidio.
